Privacy policy

For the Android game. Updated 19 October 2026.

Your data stays yours.

Knights of the Astral Plane does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal or device data.

Entirely offline.

The game works entirely offline and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, cloud services, or third-party advertising or analytics services.

Progress and settings.

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device only. The developer does not receive or retain this data and cannot restore or delete it remotely.

Users can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

No sale or sharing.

Since the developer does not collect personal data, no personal data is retained, sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Contact.
